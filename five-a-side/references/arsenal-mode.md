# Arsenal mode (optional)

The review policy stays the same. Arsenal mode changes only the presentation,
so a team can bring a little red-and-white matchday energy to a serious review.
Keep it opt-in and professional; role slugs, decisions, evidence, and gates
remain machine-readable and generic.

| Review concept | Optional matchday phrase |
| --- | --- |
| Selected reviewers | Starting XI |
| Deterministic plan | Match plan |
| Direct evidence check | VAR check |
| Focused verification after a fix | Halftime reset |
| Final decision | Final whistle |
| Human acknowledgement | Captain's armband |

Display names may use Arsenal references such as **Ødegaard**, **Bergkamp**,
**Rice**, **Raya**, **Henry**, **Mertesacker**, and **Wenger**, as long as the
stable role slug appears beside them. Never make a finding less precise for the
sake of a football metaphor.

Do not include Arsenal logos, club marks, player likenesses, match footage,
private supporter information, or claims that imply endorsement. Plain text,
colour-neutral Markdown, and the user's own opt-in vocabulary are enough to
carry the vibe.
